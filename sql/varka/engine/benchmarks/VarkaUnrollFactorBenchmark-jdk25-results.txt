================================================================================================
Milestone 4 open question 4 (VARKA-25): does an unroll factor above 1 pay?
================================================================================================

Two chain depths (8, the named short candidate; 20, the dayofweek-shaped long candidate) times
three outer-loop unroll factors (K = 1, 2, 4), each over one N=4096 row buffer (one Spark
COLUMN_BATCH_SIZE). Every K variant is fully straight-line unrolled source (no runtime loop over
the op index) so K=1 and K>1 differ only in how many independent lane groups are interleaved per
outer-loop body, not in loop-control shape. See VarkaUnrollFactorBenchmark.java.

This file is the forked-JVM measurement that replaced four in-process (forks=0) runs, which are
in git history at a092f66d096 with their own "Reading these numbers". The in-process harness
measured every benchmark on a JVM the JUnit suites and the other benchmarks had already shaped
(m4/PLAN.md, debt register, "measures a degraded JIT state"). Nothing in this file moved
by more than the earlier runs' own spread: a plain add/sub chain is the one shape that harness
measured faithfully.

OpenJDK 64-Bit Server VM 25.0.4+7-1-26.04-Ubuntu on Linux 7.0.0-30-generic
AMD Ryzen AI 9 HX PRO 370 w/ Radeon 890M (AVX-512 on a 256-bit datapath), machine otherwise idle
JMH 1.37, throughput mode, forks=1 (one forked JVM per benchmark, launched from the surefire
JVM with its argLine; the narrow run's fork inherits -XX:MaxVectorSize=16), 1 thread,
3 x 2 s warmup, 5 x 2 s measurement. Higher is better.
Run: ./build/mvn -f sql/varka/engine/pom.xml test -Dvarka.jmh=true

--- Reading these numbers (ratios by minimum-of-5) ---

* Depth 8 (short chain): flat across K = 1, 2, 4 at both widths - K=2 and K=4 sit 3% and 4%
  under K=1 at AVX-512 by min (4832 / 4694 / 4621) and within 1% at 128-bit (2262 / 2253 /
  2248), i.e. inside this file's noise and with no consistent direction. The honest null
  hypothesis in m4/PLAN.md section 2.3 holds: C2 plus the out-of-order engine already
  collect the available overlap on a body this short.
* Depth 20 (dayofweek-shaped long chain): K=2 wins at both widths - +4.4% at AVX-512 (1596 to
  1666 by min; +6.1% by mean) and +4.3% at 128-bit (716 to 747). K=4 adds nothing consistent
  over K=2: +3.4% at AVX-512 by min (1723) and -1.9% at 128-bit (733), the sign changing with
  the width. The benefit saturates at K=2, which is also the cheaper choice against
  GROUP_BUDGET.
* Net for VARKA-25: unchanged from the in-process runs. "K pays only on the long chains" holds,
  and the planner's ceiling is K=2 for a chain this long, not an unbounded search.
* Methodology note: an earlier version of this benchmark measured K=2/K=4 through a small
  constant-bound runtime for-loop over the op index, rather than the fully unrolled straight-line
  source above. That version showed K=4 losing 30-60% at some shapes - an artifact of comparing a
  loop-shaped baseline against a straight-line K=1, not a real unrolling cost. Discarded before
  being trusted; see the `SKILLS.md` entry this measurement added.
