## Summary

Of 54 pairs, 25 make some kernel neither single makes (new > 0), but most of those are two
changes sitting side by side in one class. 12 pairs pass the stricter test (beyond > 0), where a
method's body under the pair is neither single's although one single left that method alone. No
pair moves a shape that both singles leave at the defaults (unseen is 0 everywhere).

- Validity layout: 7 of 10 pairs make new kernels, 4 beyond the union. Top by beyond:
  denseValidityOnce=false + validityOrFirst=false 794,
  denseValidityOnce=false + validityByWord=true 785,
  validityByBitmap=false + validityByWord=true 604,
  validityByBitmap=false + validityOrFirst=false 526.
  The two with validityOrFirst=false are that option over part of its subject. The matrix runs
  it over the whole subject (denseValidityOnce=false,validityByBitmap=false), a different
  configuration; whether these two reach bytes that one does not was not measured. The two
  with validityByWord=true are in no configuration.
- Division and lanes: 2 of 9 pairs make new kernels (division=DOUBLE_RECIP or DOUBLE_DIV with
  mulHiDivide=false, 52 each), none beyond the union. One at a time is enough here.
- Grouping and size: 16 of 35 pairs make new kernels, 8 beyond the union, all on fuzz shapes.
  Top by beyond: groupBudget=64 + methodByteBudget=0 182, groupBudget=800 + methodByteBudget=0
  172, callSiteBudget=0 + groupLocalSlots=false 172, predictGrouping=false +
  groupLocalSlots=false 172. The largest new count, methodByteBudget=0 + driverOutputTable=false
  1474, is 0 beyond: composition only.

For the matrix: one at a time covers division and lanes. Pairs worth a configuration, on bytes
alone: denseValidityOnce=false,validityByWord=true and validityByBitmap=false,validityByWord=true
in the validity group, perhaps the two validityOrFirst=false pairs (see above);
groupBudget=800,methodByteBudget=0 and callSiteBudget=0,groupLocalSlots=false in grouping. Bytes
that differ are reach, not faults: whether these configurations find faults the singles miss
is a matrix run's question. Not measured: pairs across groups, triples, and count options at
values other than their audit arms.
