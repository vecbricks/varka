VarkaHarnessPlans.projections halves a list that does not come out as one entry an expression
over the scan, down to the expressions planned alone, where it planned the whole list alone.

Why the partition oracle's filters are not planned together: one Filter holding them would have
the optimizer merge and reorder their conjuncts. A UNION ALL of the four filters keeps each
condition as it is alone - a filter's condition is rewritten from the filter and the scan below
it - and was built, held equal by a parity test, and timed: on one fixture, ANSI off, the 164
predicate rows, third warm round,
  four filters planned alone          3.08 ms a row (0.77 each)
  their UNION ALL, one run            2.93 ms a row
  one projection                      0.58 ms
  four projections in one list        0.90 ms
An optimizer run's cost is in the plan's nodes, which a union of four filters multiplies, and
not in the run, so the union saved 5% of the oracle's planning and was dropped; a projection
list adds entries, not nodes, which is why lists pay.
