What it counts. An answer check is a line outside a helper's body that compares a session's
rows (checkAnswer, sameRows, the shared traits' checkSparkAnswer* and checkSparkRaisesTheSame, a
collect() beside an assertion, or a call of any helper whose body makes one). A check in a loop
counts once an element where the source sizes the collection, and 10 times a loop where it does
not. A session suite is flagged past 40 checks, or when it registers tests in a loop over a
collection the source does not size. A test headed by `// varka-session-lint: allow <reason>`
is not counted; a marker without a reason is itself a finding.
