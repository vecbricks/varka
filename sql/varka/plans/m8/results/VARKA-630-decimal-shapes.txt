deterministic emitter, not a benchmark); its source is VARKA-630-decimal-shapes.scratch.txt beside

The node kinds are BitOp and IntArith, about half each (the four-word set holds 164 BitOp, 136
IntArith, 14 ColumnRef and 4 WordConst); the overflow modes of the IntArith nodes were not read.

What it says for the cap: the multiplication alone is 23 levels high at its top word, so a cap of
16 refuses it and a cap of 24 or more admits it; the row's own shape wraps it in a magnitude, a
sign and a comparison (`VARKA-363.md`), which is what takes the five rows to the 26 to 60 the plan
quotes. What it says for the byte budget: the largest method is 1381 bytes for all four words
together, under a fifth of the default 8000, so the byte budget does not bind this row.

Row 363, the scale-down (`ScaleDown` lowered by `VarkaDecimalLowering.lower`, as the branch's
`VarkaScaleDownSuite` builds it), on the branch `varka-363a3-spike` (head ed152a92100) with the same
temporary change and `methodByteBudget=60000`; the scratch test is
VARKA-630-scaledown-shapes.scratch.txt. A rung is a row of `VarkaDecimalScale.BOUNDS`, k the digits
dropped; heights count a leaf as 1 (the analysis counts it as 0, so each is one more than the cap
it needs):

k = 18 gives the same as k = 6 at every rung. The top word of rung 5 alone is 3 nodes (the other three were not measured one by one). So the
scale-down alone needs a cap of 14 (rung 0, k = 1) to 27 (rung 5, k = 6) by the analysis's count,
and the largest method is 1661 bytes, a fifth of the default byte budget.

Where the SQL path stands on these branches: with the cap constant raised, the 363 branch's
compiler fuses a comparison of two decimal(38, 10) columns (height 5, 10 nodes, 961 bytes) and
none of: `d1 + d2`, `d1 - d2`, `d1 * d2`, `d1 / d2`, `d1 % d2`, any cast to or from decimal(38, 10),
`round`, `abs` or unary minus over one, all declined for "precision above 18 does not fit the long
a slot's low word holds, and its two-word store is row 359's" or, for the casts, for a decimal cast
with a floating side or an unsupported cast (scratch: VARKA-630-decimal-sql.scratch.txt). So the
compiler arms of 362b, 363, 364 and 366 are not built on these branches, and no SQL expression
reaches the networks above; they were emitted from the IR directly.

Not measured: rows 363b, 364 and 366. 363b is not a row of its own in the plan. 364's branch adds
no IR node (its tables and proofs only) and 366's adds two small ones (`LeadingZeros`,
`ReinterpretLane`), with the networks the rows would build from them not yet written, and neither
plan states a depth for them; the "26 to 60" of row 620 is from a read-only investigation whose
numbers are not recorded in the repository. The wrapping the rows add around these networks (the
magnitude, the sign, the comparison) was not added, so that figure is not reproduced. Class size
(38 KB for the four-word multiplication) was measured and not interpreted.
