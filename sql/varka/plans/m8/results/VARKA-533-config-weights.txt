== the planner
A suite's whole run under a configuration takes the file's seconds for the pair where it lists
one, and the per-suite weight times the configuration's factor otherwise; the reach share
(at least a tenth) applies on top of either, as before. Pairs are keyed by the run directory
dev/varka_matrix.sh names (every character outside [A-Za-z0-9=_.-] made `_`), so a combined
configuration's commas match the file's underscores. Self-tests: 12 of 12 pass, 3 new (a measured
pair over the weight times the factor and the fallback where none is listed, the share on a
measured pair, the per-configuration output holding only whole runs and read back by a comma
configuration).
