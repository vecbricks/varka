VARKA-603 diagnosis: the checked TINYINT multiply's store at 128 bits under AVX2
====================================================================================

Taken 11 November 2026 on the laptop (AMD Ryzen AI 9 HX PRO 370, which has AVX-512BW), JDK
25.0.5 (Ubuntu build), master d241c12372d, no compiler edits, every JVM forced to
-XX:UseAVX=2 -XX:MaxVectorSize=16. The rates below are diagnostic only: they come from the
laptop under forced flags, beside an sbt build, and decide nothing. The row's evidence is the
EPYC 7763 runner (VARKA-603.md 4).

HotSpot's output
----------------

The standard (non-OSR) level-4 compiles of h's and i's loops, NarrowEmit6N::loopDense0 (442
bytes) and NarrowEmit7N::loopMasked0 (706 bytes), and their WIDENED twins 6W and 7W, are each
followed by

    ** not supported: op=storeMasked vlen=8 etype=byte mismatched_ms=0

and each inline tree has exactly two declined VectorSupport calls, one per int group of the
iteration:

    @ 33   jdk.internal.vm.vector.VectorSupport::storeMasked (40 bytes)
           failed to inline: failed to inline (intrinsic)

Every other VectorSupport call in those trees reads "(intrinsic)": load, convert (the B2I parts
and the I2B), binaryOp, compare, fromBitsCoerced, test, and the store's mask,
indexPartiallyInUpperRange (from ByteVector.SPECIES_64.indexInRange(0, lanes)). The mask is
intrinsified; the predicated store is refused. "the arms' class bytes are identical" is printed
for h, i, j, k and l: both arms emit the same kernel for them. The loops of f and j have no
storeMasked call and no refusal.

Allocation (-Xlog:gc young pauses per case, three rungs): h 235, i 218; a 3, b 7, c 5, e 9,
f 14, g 16, j 4, k 4, l 19. The JDK-only model (Repro603 masked) reads 20 bytes a row at
UseAVX=2 and 0 at the host's own AVX-512.

Side readings (not this row's; VARKA-603.md 10.1)
---------------------------------------------

- The OSR compiles of i (7N and 7W) also print "** unbox failed v2=Phi" and
  "** not supported: arity=1 op=cast#513/4 vlen1=2 etype2=byte" for a
  Long128Vector::bitsToShuffle convert. The standard compile that runs has neither.
- "not supported: op=storeMasked vlen=16 etype=byte" is printed after standalone compiles of
  jdk.incubator.vector.ByteVector::intoMemorySegment (99 bytes), not after a kernel's loop:
  a 16-lane masked byte store. A byte-driven kernel's epilogue at 128 bits stores that way,
  but the caller was not identified, so that attribution is inferred.
