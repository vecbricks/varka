laptop skip share (the share of its tests a configuration skips, averaged over the 34). This is
a derivation, not a measurement: it weighs every test of a suite alike. Cost = laptop recorded
seconds less plain (negative costs counted as 0 in sums). CI's catalyst defaults over the laptop's

### Why the built branch was break-even

Its kept set cost 743.3 s net on the laptop (VARKA-518-catalyst-reach.txt), about 820 JVM-s on
CI at the 1.1 ratio, over catalyst's three jobs of two JVMs: at least 2.3 minutes a JVM if
spread evenly, and VarkaGroupingBoundSuite's 267.0 s alone, about 4.9 minutes on CI, lands in
one JVM of one job. It saved 12559 matrix seconds a run (that file), 105 lane minutes, 3.5
minutes of matrix wall at 30 slots. So 2.3 to 4.9 minutes on the Build's critical path (section
1) bought 3.5 off the matrix: about even, and the Build's minutes come first, before the matrix
is dispatched. No CI run of branch varka-518-catalyst-reach exists (gh api runs?branch= returns
none); the hold is this derivation from the laptop numbers.

### The fix: record a suite only where it pays

Eight suites carry most of the cost: VarkaGroupingBoundSuite 267.0, VarkaVariantSuite 149.3,
VarkaEmitterBudgetSuite 53.7, VarkaShapeCacheSuite 53.6, VarkaMaskOfWordSuite 50.8,
VarkaEmitterFramesSuite 42.8, VarkaEmitterSplitDriverSuite 33.1, VarkaFamilySwitchSuite 32.6:
683.9 s of the 901.9, for 2601 of the 11572 saved. Their recording re-emits large kernels once
per covered configuration (34 times), which a cheaper recorder cannot avoid without the options-
read design row 518 closed (VARKA-518-reads-local.txt).

Rule: a suite records where saved / cost >= 20, or where its laptop recording cost is at most

## 4. Where the recording could run

(a) The matrix's build-and-plan job, recording catalyst's defaults once. That job takes 1.3 to
1.9 minutes today (section 2) and every bin waits on its plan. Catalyst's defaults are 1688.2
JUnit-s on CI before any recording; on the job's two JVMs at least 14 minutes added before the
first bin, for 2.3 to 3.5 minutes saved. Recording instead in the matrix's own defaults catalyst
units, planning catalyst's configuration bins after them, puts a barrier of one bin (about eight
minutes at the 480 s lane) plus a second plan job before catalyst's configurations start, while
sql's 19558 JUnit-s (163 lane minutes, about 5.4 minutes of 30 slots) are all that fill the
slots meanwhile: about 4.6 minutes of idle slots for 2.3 saved, and two matrix jobs whose
max-parallel must sum to 30. Both lose.

(b) The fork's master Build. Nothing waits on it: it runs after each merge, eight successful
runs on 30 October in section 1's table. It can record every catalyst suite but the five past
the watchdog (laptop cost 88.2 + 1891.1 + 423.3 = 2402.6 s, about 44 minutes of JVM time on CI
over six JVMs, VarkaEmitCostSuite's 638.8 s in one of them, so about 12 minutes on its longest
job), holding catalyst's three slots that much longer a merge. Its record is the same commit's
for a matrix of master (row 522's weekly run). For a pull request's matrix it is valid where the
diff from the record's commit (an ancestor of the head) changes nothing catalyst builds

(c) The PR Build's catalyst jobs, with the rule of section 3: about 16 s a JVM, inside the
