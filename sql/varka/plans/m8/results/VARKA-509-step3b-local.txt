# Row 509 step 3, its second part, on the laptop, 29 October 2026: VarkaDifferentialSuite's
# hand-written date, interval and integer shapes moved onto the harness as
# VarkaDifferentialHarnessSuite. dev/varka_matrix.sh over the suites, one JVM at a time; "before"

Counts: of the suite's 94 tests, 67 move to the harness, 26 stay end to end, and 1 is in both
(its answers in the harness, the ILLEGAL_DAY_OF_WEEK a user sees end to end, a condition no
coverage row raises). The harness suite has 68 tests, the session suite 27; each keeps the old
name.

What a harness query checks, beside every answer being Spark's under both consumers (the row
node's for the fused outputs) or through the filter evaluator (over the conjuncts the compiler
fuses, which is what the rule hands the Varka node), in the old test's terms:
  RUNS       something fused and a kernel answered a batch: checkDifferential's expectFused
  FUSED      something fused: assertFused alone
  WHOLE      fused whole and a kernel answered: the old "fused, not a residual Filter"
  SERVED     fused whole, every batch answered: the old numFallbackBatchesDeclined === 0
             beside a kernel run
  DECLINES   fused whole and a kernel declined a batch by its own status: the old
             numFallbackBatchesDeclined > 0 with numFallbackBatchesKernel === 0
  NOT_FUSED  nothing fused: checkDifferential's expectFused = false
  ANSWERS    the answers only
and, where the old test read verbose EXPLAIN, the same lines from VarkaFusionReport ("far:
residual (day range [", "y: fused", "kernel 2 of 2").

What stays end to end: the plan around the Varka nodes (a narrowing projection absorbed, the
residual conjunct's row filter and the order Spark evaluates conjuncts in, a projection stacked
on a filter's compacted batch, the common-expression hoist), the fallback hooks and their
metrics, the reference arm of guardDayProducers (asserted on the metric, never on the value,
which the harness would compare), AQE, aggregation, the cache's batches and a cached view of
fused work, tasks sharing a kernel class, a non-Arrow source, the SQL UI's residual count,
Metaspace and the shape cache's counts, and the next_day error.

Two of the old tables changed shape in the move. last_day's two far-future rows are a table of
their own: past the range the calendar lowering is exact over, they decline their batch, as
they did in the old suite's last partition, and the in-range rows are a table the kernel
answers. And a query the old test stacked on a filter is the filter and the projection, each
over the whole table.

New: VarkaHarnessParitySuite plans every hand-written query of both harness suites in a session,
under its part's ANSI mode, and finds each output or condition equal to the harness's.
