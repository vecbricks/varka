With five probes and four slots the fifth ran alone in a second wave. Five at once on the 24 cores
caused no timeout of the 600 s a child may take; the default is now one probe at once for every
four cores up to five. Not measured: any machine with fewer cores than 24 (a four-core runner runs
one at a time, as before), and five at once on a loaded machine.

The skip: `VARKA_REGEN_BASE=HEAD dev/varka_regen_generated.sh --all` on a tree whose changes against
that base touch nothing the JVM steps read took 0.6 s of wall clock, wrote the push check's marker
and changed no file. The same script on this branch, whose own changes are inputs, printed "running
the JVM steps: the input dev/varka_regen_generated.sh changed". The skip assumes master's generated
files are current; `--full` runs the steps whatever the decision says.
