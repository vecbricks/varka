VARKA-630 part 2b: the time of the lane lifts on a shared condition chain, before and after a
memo by identity.

Date: 21 November 2026, one laptop, one JVM, a scratch test run once per cell (single readings,
not a benchmark); its source is VARKA-630-lane-lifts.scratch.txt beside this file.

The shape: a condition chain where level k is `level(k-1) AND NOT level(k-1)`, over one
comparison of two SHORT columns, so it has 2^k paths over k + 1 distinct nodes.
`VarkaConditionCompiler.onIntLane` and `onLongLane` lift it to the int and the long lane.

Before (no memo), from an earlier scratch test of the same shape on the #1591 branch head
dc9fd060c32, whose lifts have no memo (its source is the same loop, with equals added):

  depth  onIntLane   onLongLane
  8      9 ms        1 ms
  16     11 ms       23 ms
  20     121 ms      213 ms
  22     578 ms      807 ms
  24     2521 ms     3194 ms

Each two levels multiplied the time by four. The same run compared two separate lifts with
record equals: 56 ms at depth 16, 1724 ms at 22, and over 30000 ms at 24 (part 2a's
VarkaVectorIR.sameStructure is the fix for that comparison, not a memo).

After (identity memo, the copies of a shared condition are one object):

  depth  onIntLane   onLongLane
  16     13593 us    763 us
  24     178 us      280 us
  32     248 us      400 us
  48     291 us      174 us
  64     163 us      189 us

The 16-level int-lane reading is the first call and includes class loading. The third lift,
`VarkaIntegralCastCompiler.onLane`, has the same shape and the same memo; it was not timed
separately. `VarkaChronoCompiler.rearm` rebuilds a value chain, not a condition, and was neither
changed nor measured.

The before column is a single reading from a run whose source is not committed (it is this
loop plus a record-equals comparison of two lifts), so it cannot be reproduced from this branch.

Not measured: a condition that is not a chain, and any query that reaches a shared condition.
Whether Catalyst boolean expressions compile to a shared condition is not shown here.
