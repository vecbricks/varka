The kernel warm-up's verdict under a starved machine (VARKA-221.md 2 and 9):
VarkaKernelWarmupSuite run as plain JVMs pinned to cores 2 and 3 of the laptop while two busy
loops hold both cores - the starvation sql/varka/skills/the-jit.md records as reproducing a
four-core runner's compile delays. A probe's readings, not a benchmark: what they establish is
which method allocates and whether the suite passes, and the warm-up durations are read from
the warm-up's own log line ("is compiled after a warm-up of N ms").

Reproduce:
  taskset -c 2 bash -c 'while :; do :; done' &   (and the same on core 3)
  taskset -c 2,3 java --add-modules jdk.incubator.vector --enable-native-access=ALL-UNNAMED \
    -Dspark.testing=1 -cp <catalyst test classpath> org.scalatest.tools.Runner -oW \
    -s org.apache.spark.sql.catalyst.expressions.codegen.varka.VarkaKernelWarmupSuite
